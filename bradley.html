About Bradley

Bradley Babcock is an owner, operations manager, and Journeyman Electrician at Heico Electric. Builders and property owners value Bradley for his steady approach, clean workmanship, and dependable follow-through on the jobsite.

He is especially focused on the details that matter: neat panel work, organized rough-ins, thoughtful layout execution, and troubleshooting that gets to the real issue.

Bradley brings a calm, professional presence that helps jobs run smoother from start to finish. He’s the kind of electrician clients trust to show up prepared, do clean work, and keep the job moving.

His work reflects the standard Heico Electric wants to be known for, reliability, professionalism, and quality that holds up over time.
