About Mitchell

Mitchell Heidegger is the founder and owner behind Heico Electric. He leads projects with a focus on safety, clean workmanship, and electrical systems that are installed the right way.

From commercial projects to custom homes and service upgrades, Mitchell brings practical field knowledge, strong communication, and a commitment to doing the job right the first time.

His approach is simple: show up, stay organized, communicate clearly, and deliver work that builders and property owners can count on.

Mitchell believes the best electrical contractors are the ones who make the job easier for everyone else — cleaner installs, fewer surprises, and dependable follow-through from start to finish.
